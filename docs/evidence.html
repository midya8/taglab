01 / AN EVENT IS A SET OF COMPLETE PATHS

What is allowed?

At each position, allow a subset of A, B and C. Their Cartesian product defines the event. These are your hypotheses, not labels recovered from the observations. No stored true labels are shown here.

Comma-separated. One to 256 symbols. Editing them changes the observed input, not a training example.

Use * for A/B/C, A or BC for subsets, and - for an empty subset. Separate positions with spaces. Duplicate letters and a wrong number of positions are rejected.

Zero displayed probability can mean floating-point underflow. A finite log probability and nonempty support still define a possible event. An empty subset instead has exactly zero support: no conditional marginals or path are returned.

The pale bars are unrestricted node marginals. The red bars condition on your entire label event, including restrictions at other positions. A longer bar is not evidence that the model became more accurate. Conditional entropy can increase or decrease.

03 / KEEP THE JOINT DISTRIBUTION IN VIEW

Two adjacent labels.

Rows are FROM labels; columns are TO labels. Summing a row gives the left node marginal; summing a column gives the right. A forbidden label has exactly zero mass, not a very negative finite score.

05 / DRAW ONE WHOLE PATH, NOT INDEPENDENT NODES
A sample has a history.
The same label event above defines the distribution. Backward sampling starts at the final label, then draws each predecessor given its already drawn successor. Drawing every node independently matches node marginals in ideal arithmetic, but generally loses pair dependence. Both methods below receive the same uniforms as an inspectable coupling, not independent experimental groups.
Uniform at index t is used for position t, although positions execute in reverse order. Each value must be finite and in [0,1). A manual list produces one draw. Seeds are integers 1 through 4294967295.

These are finite-precision samples from a fixed toy model, not new labels, model training or uncertainty about fitted parameters. The portable PRNG gives deterministic 32-bit mid-bin uniforms, not certified independent randomness or cryptographic entropy. Very small probabilities can underflow or have no representable CDF interval; traces flag underflowed labels and collapsed CDF bins. Frequencies from 64 draws are descriptive, not a test or confidence interval. Exact pair dependence is computed separately from the model.
06 / CONDITIONING IS NOT CONSTRAINED TRAINING
A narrower question.
Not a new claim.
This page supports position-wise label subsets only, not arbitrary regular-language rules, global counts or transition exclusions. It uses the existing six fitted models without retraining or selecting on these queries. Different fitted families also differ in their fitted weights and penalty geometry; their display is descriptive, not an isolated causal comparison.
Under the independent-label model, restricting one position leaves other unrestricted node marginals unchanged, up to roundoff. A chain can propagate the information through its path distribution. Neither behavior establishes real-world annotation quality or calibrated confidence.
Inference is a finite-score log-space sum over the exact allowed support, O(T × K²). “Exact” describes dynamic programming, not exact real-number arithmetic. Original synthetic observations, no text corpus, external model service or paid API. The original losing test metric and budget-exhausted fits remain on the training page.
